python3 << 'EOF'
def leed_v41_bdc_score_target(target_level='Gold'):
    target_pts = {'Certified': 40, 'Silver': 50, 'Gold': 60, 'Platinum': 80}
    print(f"Target: LEED v4.1 BD+C {target_level} — {target_pts[target_level]} pts")
    cats = [
        ('LT', 16, 'Location + Transport'),
        ('SS', 10, 'Sustainable Sites'),
        ('WE', 11, 'Water Efficiency'),
        ('EA', 33, 'Energy + Atmosphere'),
        ('MR', 13, 'Materials + Resources'),
        ('EQ', 16, 'Indoor Environmental Quality'),
        ('IN',  6, 'Innovation'),
        ('RP',  4, 'Regional Priority'),
    ]
    print()
    print(f"{'Cat':<4} {'Max':<5} {'Target':<7} Description")
    total = sum(m for _, m, _ in cats)
    for code, maxp, desc in cats:
        target = int(target_pts[target_level] / total * maxp + 0.5)
        print(f"{code:<4} {maxp:<5} {target:<7} {desc}")
    print(f"\nTotal max: {total}; target: {target_pts[target_level]}")

leed_v41_bdc_score_target('Gold')
EOF

### 6. Anti-patterns

- Pursuing LEED Platinum on tight budget — push back; achievable cost-premium ceiling.
- WELL features without performance verification at POE — fail re-cert.
- PHIUS w/o CPHC consultant from SD — almost impossible to certify retroactively.
- Specifying "LEED-compliant" materials without verifying EPD / HPD / Declare.
- Treating CALGreen as voluntary in CA — mandatory; AHJ rejects.
- Skipping ENERGY STAR baseline year for NYC LL97 — forfeits flexibility.
- Daylight via single-orientation simulation — sDA + ASE both required.
- Carpet w/o CRI Green Label Plus in spec — LEED EQc4 miss.
- Forgetting low-emitting adhesives / sealants / paints / coatings.
- LEED EAc1 model based on rule of thumb — Appendix G PRM is strict.
- Hospitality LEED Silver assumed; brand standard often Gold or higher.
- LBC Equity Petal ignored — Just label requirement embedded; whole-firm.

### 7. Edge cases

- **Existing buildings**: LEED v4.1 O+M; performance-period documentation; ASHRAE 100 retro.
- **Multifamily affordable + LIHTC**: HUD MAP Healthy Homes; LEED for Homes; Enterprise Green Communities; PHIUS CORE.
- **Healthcare**: WELL + LEED + FGI 2022; Green Guide for Health Care preceded LEED Healthcare.
- **K-12 + Higher Ed**: LEED for Schools; CHPS (Collaborative for High Performance Schools); WELL Education.
- **Federal projects**: GSA P100 mandates LEED Gold; DOE Federal Bldg Performance Std.
- **NYC LL97 timeline**: 2024 Tier 1; 2030 Tier 2; 2050 net-zero.
- **CA TSEC + Title 24 net-zero trajectory**: 2030 commercial.
- **WELL + LEED double-cert**: shared submittals possible via aligned template.
- **PHIUS small project**: < 4,000 sf simpler path; CPHC still recommended.
- **LBC Living certification**: requires Net-Positive operation for 12 mo; rare.
- **EPD availability gap**: major brands lag for finishes; force-spec via performance.
- **Embodied carbon mandates**: NY CHPS Act; Buy Clean CA; Buy Clean Marin.
